---
title: "Resume Variants"
description: "AI-tailored resume variants for specific job applications"
---

Variants are tailored versions of your master profile, stored as delta overrides.

## How it works

1. Click **New Variant** → paste job description + company/role
2. AI reads JD + your master profile → suggests improvements to descriptions and impact statements
3. Review each suggestion (side-by-side diff) → accept or reject
4. Saved as a named variant (e.g. `principal-consultant-accenture`)

## Key design decision

Variants store **only the overrides** (changed fields), not a full copy of your profile. When rendered, the master record is merged with overrides at read time. This means **updating your master profile automatically flows through to all variants**.
